# Training Request Form

## Purpose

This form is used to request training for Swedwise staff. It ensures that training investments are justified, aligned with business needs and IMS competence requirements, and properly approved before commitment.

## Instructions

1. **Complete Sections 1-4**: Employee or manager completes request details
2. **Justify**: Clearly explain business need and expected outcomes
3. **Submit for Approval**: Route to manager and Resource Management
4. **Await Decision**: Approval required before booking training
5. **Post-Training**: Complete evaluation (SW-IMS-FRM-009) and update competence records

## Notes for Users

### When to Submit a Training Request

Submit a training request when:

- You identify a competence gap affecting job performance
- You need certification/qualification for a customer project
- New technology or process requires formal training
- Regulatory or contractual requirements mandate training
- Career development opportunity arises aligned with business needs

### Approval Timeframes

Target approval timeframes:

- **Standard Request**: 5 working days
- **Urgent Request**: 2 working days (subject to availability)
- **High-Cost Request (>50k SEK)**: 10 working days (requires CEO approval)

**Plan ahead**: Submit requests at least 4 weeks before desired training date to allow for approval and booking.

### What Makes a Strong Request

**Strong justification includes:**

- Clear link between training and business need
- Specific competence gap or requirement
- Measurable outcomes/benefits
- Realistic cost-benefit analysis
- Evidence of due diligence (alternatives considered)

**Weak justification:**

- "This looks interesting"
- "Everyone else is getting training"
- Generic "professional development" without specifics

### Cost Guidelines

**Typical Approved Training Costs:**

- **Technical Certifications**: 10,000 - 30,000 SEK
- **Industry Conferences**: 15,000 - 50,000 SEK (including travel)
- **Multi-day Courses**: 20,000 - 60,000 SEK
- **Online Courses**: 1,000 - 10,000 SEK
- **Webinars**: 0 - 5,000 SEK

**Cost thresholds may vary based on business criticality and ROI.**

### Training Budget

Swedwise allocates training budget based on:

- **Individual entitlement**: [TBD - e.g., 15,000 SEK per employee annually]
- **Departmental budgets**: Managed by department heads
- **Strategic initiatives**: Separate budget for company-wide priorities

**Budget availability does not guarantee approval**—business justification is still required.

### Post-Training Expectations

After completing approved training, employees should:

1. **Complete evaluation** (SW-IMS-FRM-009) within 1 week
2. **Share knowledge** with team (presentation, documentation, lunch & learn)
3. **Apply learning** in work activities
4. **Update competence records** with new skills/certifications
5. **Provide evidence** of effectiveness (e.g., successfully leading projects, reduced errors)

### Declined Requests

If your request is declined:

- **Understand why**: Request feedback from approver
- **Consider alternatives**: Free resources, internal training, mentoring
- **Resubmit later**: If circumstances change or stronger justification emerges
- **Discuss with manager**: Explore other development opportunities

Common decline reasons:

- Insufficient business justification
- Budget constraints
- Timing conflicts
- Alternative solutions available
- Not aligned with current business priorities
