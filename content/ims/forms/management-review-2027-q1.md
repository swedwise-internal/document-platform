# Management Review 2027 Q1 — Draft Pack

**Document ID**: SW-IMS-REC-001-v1.1
**Review ID**: MR-2027-Q1
**Effective Date**: [TBD]
**Owner**: IMS Owner (COO)
**Approved by**: [TBD — CEO as chair, after the review]

This is the pack for Swedwise's **first** management review under SW-IMS-PRO-004. It was drafted on
2026-10-29, before any record exists, so that the review has a tested agenda and an honest picture of
where the management system stands. Everything marked "none yet" is a true statement on the drafting date.
The IMS Owner updates the pack two weeks before the meeting (SW-IMS-PRO-004 section 8.1) and replaces
each state with the figures of that day.

**Summary for the chair.** All the documents describe the management system, but no records yet show that
Swedwise runs it. Every clause of the ISO Harmonized Structure is at least partly described in a draft
document (15 described, 11 partly). None is met, because nothing is approved, no role has a named person,
no audit has been held before 2027 and nothing is measured. This first review is mainly a review of
decisions. Its main output is the decisions in section 3.

## 3. Decisions the review must take

Proposals are drafted in session. The Management Team decides. Each decision taken becomes an action in
SW-IMS-FRM-006 with an owner job and a due date.

| ID | Decision | Proposal | Action owner (job) if decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **Approve the IMS documents**, all at once or in stages | Approve in stages: first the IMS policy, the manual (clauses 4–10) and the eight procedures the system cannot run without (document control, risk assessment, internal audit, management review, nonconformity, monitoring and measurement, competence, communication); the rest by the Q2 review. Each approval sets effective and review dates | COO (IMS Owner) |
| D-02 | **Appoint IMS roles to named people**, recorded in the manual (SW-IMS-MAN-005) and the role descriptions | Confirm the jobs settled 2026-10-29: IMS Owner = COO; Quality Lead = Head of PMO & CI; Environmental Lead = CFO; Service Delivery Manager, Customer Success Lead, Resource Management Lead = CSO; Customer Acquisition and Customer Development Lead = CCO; IT Manager = COO; HR Lead = HR / People & Culture; continual improvement = Head of PMO & CI. **Decide** the two that are only proposed: CISO = COO, and communication owner = CEO. Then name the person in each job. Record the Privacy Lead = CEO with the CFO as stand-in (not a GDPR DPO, none required — owner 2026-10-29; never the same person as the CISO); decide who holds the Risk Manager role | CEO |
| D-03 | **Confirm the scope** of the IMS (SW-IMS-MAN-004 4.3) | Re-read against today's company: units, offices [to confirm], services sold (consulting, licences, support, and which SaaS services, if any), hosting partners, and the 2026 editions of ISO 9001 and 14001. Decide which standards to certify against and in which order | COO |
| D-04 | **Update the organisation in the manual** | Replace the 2025 organisation chart in SW-IMS-MAN-005 with the 2026 units and jobs from CompanyIT `organisation.toml` | COO |
| D-05 | **One owner for communication** | CEO (proposed in session), instead of "the management team" | CEO |
| D-06 | **One nonconformity procedure** | Confirm the proposal of 2026-10-29: SW-IMS-PRO-005 is the one procedure (eight steps, with 8D as the root-cause method for major and critical nonconformities) and SW-IMS-PRO-009 is withdrawn; fix the registers' IDs at the same time | COO |
| D-07 | **Partners separate from ordinary suppliers** | Confirm the owner's line of 2026-10-29: Finance (CFO) owns ordinary purchasing; Sales (CCO) owns partners and the vendors whose products we sell. Order a partner procedure and update the procurement and supplier policies | CCO, CFO |
| D-08 | **Set the IMS objectives** (SW-IMS-MAN-006 6.2.2) | Approve objectives that can be measured. Start with the KPIs the hub and Entra already produce, and set targets only after a baseline. For each objective with no source (customer satisfaction, travel CO2, energy, e-waste, phishing, vulnerabilities), decide to build a source, buy one or drop the objective. Settle the conflicting targets listed in SW-IMS-REG-008 section 3 | CEO, with each objective owner |
| D-09 | **How customer feedback is collected** | Choose a survey method and frequency, and where complaints are logged (proposal: a complaint marking on hub support tickets, so no separate register is kept by hand) | Head of PMO & CI |
| D-10 | **Order the first risk assessment** | Run SW-IMS-PRO-002 across all 26 processes in Q2 2027, including information security risks and environmental aspects; review the Statement of Applicability after it | COO |
| D-11 | **Resources** | Approve: time for 3–5 internal auditors and their training (SW-IMS-TRN-005); an external auditor for audits A-2027-12 and A-2027-13 [to confirm: budget]; and the priority of hub release 1.3 (`management`, `reporting`, `leadership`), which would hold the IMS records instead of documents kept by hand | CEO, CFO |
| D-12 | **Approve the audit programme 2027** (SW-IMS-REG-007) | Approve, including the independence rules and the risk ratings; confirm the auditor pool | COO |
| D-13 | **Customer support procedure** | Order a support procedure owned by the CSO, separate from the security incident procedure SW-ISMS-PRO-001 | CSO |
| D-14 | **Procedures for processes without one** | Decide which processes keep the hub as their documented procedure (sales, invoicing and finance, resource allocation) and which need a written one (account management, partners and vendors, HR, strategic planning) | COO |
| D-15 | **Where IMS records live** | Decide where audit reports, nonconformities, review minutes and KPI values are kept until the hub holds them (every procedure's records table says `[TBD]`) | COO |
| D-16 | **Review frequency** | Confirm quarterly reviews (SW-IMS-PRO-004 5.1) or set two a year for the first cycle; set the next review date | CEO |
