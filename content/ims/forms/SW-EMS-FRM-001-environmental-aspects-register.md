# Environmental Aspect Assessment Form

## Use

This form assesses a **new or changed** environmental aspect before it is entered into the Environmental Aspects
Register (SW-EMS-REG-001). Use it when:
- a change goes through change management (SW-IMS-PRO-008);
- a new product, service, office or supplier is introduced;
- the register's yearly review finds an aspect that is missing.

The method is the one in SW-EMS-REG-001 section 3: score = severity × frequency, each rated 1–5. An aspect is
significant when the score is 12 or more, when a compliance obligation in SW-IMS-REG-010 applies, or when an
interested party in SW-IMS-REG-006 has a stated requirement about it. This form uses no other method.

Until version 2.0, this form carried its own scoring method and its own example rows with aspect ids that clashed with
the register. Those rows are gone; the register is the only list.
