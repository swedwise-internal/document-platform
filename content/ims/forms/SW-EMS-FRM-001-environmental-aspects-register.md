# Environmental Aspects Register Template

## Instructions for Use

**Purpose**: This register identifies environmental aspects of Swedwise's activities, evaluates their significance, and documents controls and objectives.

**How to Complete**:
1. **Identify Activities/Processes**: List all business activities (e.g., office operations, business travel, IT services delivery)
2. **Determine Environmental Aspects**: For each activity, identify what interacts with the environment (e.g., energy consumption, waste generation, travel emissions)
3. **Identify Environmental Impacts**: State the potential or actual effect on the environment (e.g., climate change, resource depletion, pollution)
4. **Assess Conditions**: Indicate whether the aspect occurs under Normal operations, Abnormal conditions (maintenance, startup), or Emergency situations
5. **Evaluate Significance**: Score against criteria (Scale, Severity, Probability, Legal compliance) - use rating scale 1-5
6. **Calculate Total Score**: Sum the criteria scores to determine overall significance
7. **Determine Significance**: Aspects with score ≥12 are considered Significant and require objectives/targets
8. **Document Controls**: List existing controls that manage or mitigate the aspect
9. **Set Objectives/Targets**: For significant aspects, reference specific environmental objectives or targets (from Objectives Register)
10. **Review**: Update register annually or when activities change significantly

**Significance Scoring Criteria** (Rate 1-5 for each):
- **Scale**: How widespread is the impact? (1=localized, 5=global)
- **Severity**: How serious is the environmental impact? (1=negligible, 5=severe)
- **Probability**: How likely is the impact to occur? (1=rare, 5=continuous)
- **Legal**: Is there legal/regulatory compliance risk? (1=no regulations, 5=strict regulations with penalties)

**Review Frequency**: At least annually, or when:
- New activities introduced
- Operations or processes change significantly
- Legal requirements change
- Significant environmental incidents occur
- Management review identifies need

**Owner**: Environmental Lead
