# Privacy Notice for Job Candidates

**Document ID**: SW-ISMS-NOT-002-v1.0
**Effective Date**: [TBD]
**Review Date**: [TBD]
**Owner**: Privacy Lead

This notice tells you, as someone who applies for a job or assignment at Swedwise, what personal data we process about
you, why, and what rights you have.

## 1. Who is responsible

**Swedwise AB** (organisation number [to confirm: organisation number], [to confirm: registered address]) is the
controller for your personal data.

Questions: **privacy@swedwise.se**. Swedwise has **no Data Protection Officer** and is not required to have one; our
Privacy Lead is responsible for data protection.

## 2. What we process and why

We use your data to assess your application, contact you, interview you, check references and decide whom to hire.

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Handling your application and interviews | Steps you ask us to take before a possible employment contract, and our legitimate interest in finding the right person |
| Checking references | Our legitimate interest; we contact referees you have named, and tell you before we do |
| Keeping your application for future roles | Only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

## 3. What data

Your name and contact details, CV, cover letter, education, experience, certifications, interview notes, references,
and anything else you choose to send us [to confirm: whether tests or background checks are used, and when].

Please do not send us sensitive data such as health information or your personal identity number unless we ask for it.

## 4. Who gets your data

- **Inside Swedwise:** the people taking part in the recruitment (HR and the hiring manager).
- **Recruitment agencies** we work with, if you applied through one [to confirm: which].
- **Our suppliers (processors)**, who handle data for us under our instructions, such as Microsoft (mail, Teams and
  files) [to confirm: recruitment tool, if any].

We do not sell your data or share it for others' marketing.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Microsoft is a US company. Where data may leave the EU/EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's
standard contractual clauses [to confirm]. You can ask us for details.

## 6. How long we keep it

If you are not hired, we keep your application for **2 years** after we have decided on the recruitment, so that we can
answer any claim under the Swedish Discrimination Act (Diskrimineringslagen) [to confirm]. Then we delete it. If you
have agreed that we may keep your application for future roles, we keep it as long as your consent says [to confirm:
period], or until you withdraw consent.

If you are hired, your application becomes part of your employee file (see the employee privacy notice).

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have data erased when we no longer need it or have no right to keep it;
- have processing restricted while a question is examined;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it;
- get data you gave us in a machine-readable format (data portability), where it applies.

Write to **privacy@swedwise.se**. We answer within one month. It is free.

## 8. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, tell us first at privacy@swedwise.se. You also have the right to complain to
the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**, www.imy.se.
