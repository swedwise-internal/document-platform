# Privacy Notice for Website Visitors and Marketing Contacts

**Document ID**: SW-ISMS-NOT-003-v1.0
**Effective Date**: [TBD]
**Review Date**: [TBD]
**Owner**: Privacy Lead

This notice tells you what personal data Swedwise processes when you visit swedwise.se, contact us, or are a business
contact we market to, and what rights you have.

## 1. Who is responsible

**Swedwise AB** (organisation number [to confirm: organisation number], [to confirm: registered address]) is the
controller for your personal data.

Questions: **privacy@swedwise.se**. Swedwise has **no Data Protection Officer** and is not required to have one; our
Privacy Lead is responsible for data protection.

## 2. What we process, why, and on what basis

| What we do | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Answering you when you use the contact form or write to us | Name, e-mail, phone, company, your message | Our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and doing business |
| Keeping in touch with business contacts (customers, prospects, partners) | Name, title, company, business contact details, our communication | Our legitimate interest in business relationships with companies and organisations |
| Sending newsletters or marketing e-mail [to confirm: whether we send a newsletter] | Name, e-mail, company, what you signed up for | Your consent, or our legitimate interest for existing business contacts; you can always say no |
| Understanding how the website is used and which companies visit it | IP address, device and browser data, pages visited, the company identified from the visit [to confirm: tools used] | Your consent for cookies that are not necessary; our legitimate interest for statistics without cookies |
| Advertising and social media | Ad and page statistics from Google Ads and our LinkedIn company page | Your consent where cookies are used; our legitimate interest in reaching business customers |

## 3. Cookies

The website uses cookies and similar technology [to confirm: which cookies the site sets, and whether a consent banner is
in place]. Cookies that are needed for the site to work are always set. Other cookies — for statistics, visitor
identification and advertising — are set only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time
[to confirm: how]. You can also block cookies in your browser.

Tools that may be in use [to confirm each]: Leadfeeder (identifies companies visiting the site), Google Ads (ads and
conversion measurement), LinkedIn (company page and its statistics).

## 4. Who gets your data

- **Inside Swedwise:** sales and marketing, and the people who answer your enquiry.
- **Our suppliers (processors)**, who handle data for us under our instructions: our website host [to confirm],
  HubSpot (customer relationship management), Microsoft (mail), Leadfeeder (website visits) [to confirm: still in use].
- **Google and LinkedIn** process some data for their own purposes as well. For statistics on our LinkedIn company page,
  Swedwise and LinkedIn are joint controllers.

We do not sell your data.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some of these suppliers are US companies (for example Google, LinkedIn's group, HubSpot and Microsoft). Where data may
leave the EU/EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses [to confirm: per
supplier]. You can ask us for details.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Enquiries and business contacts: while we have a business relationship, and **2 years** after the last contact
  [to confirm].
- Marketing consent: until you withdraw it, and a record of it for **1 year** after that, so that we can show what you
  agreed to.
- Website statistics and cookies: as stated in the cookie settings [to confirm].

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have data erased when we no longer need it or have no right to keep it;
- have processing restricted while a question is examined;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests — and **always** to say no to direct marketing;
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it;
- get data you gave us in a machine-readable format (data portability), where it applies.

Write to **privacy@swedwise.se**. We answer within one month. It is free.

## 8. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, tell us first at privacy@swedwise.se. You also have the right to complain to
the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**, www.imy.se.
