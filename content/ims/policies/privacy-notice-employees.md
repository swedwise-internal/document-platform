# Privacy Notice for Employees

**Document ID**: SW-ISMS-NOT-001-v1.0
**Effective Date**: [TBD]
**Review Date**: [TBD]
**Owner**: Privacy Lead

This notice tells you, as an employee of Swedwise, what personal data we process about you, why, and what rights you
have. It also applies to consultants and subcontractors who have a Swedwise account, where relevant.

## 1. Who is responsible

**Swedwise AB** (organisation number 556793-2354, Västra Torggatan 11, 652 25 Karlstad [to confirm: registered address; the address in signed contracts 2023–2025]) is the
controller for the personal data in this notice.

Questions about your personal data: **privacy@swedwise.se**. The mailbox is read by the Privacy Lead (our CEO) and the
stand-in (our CFO).

Swedwise has **no Data Protection Officer** and is not required to have one. The Privacy Lead is responsible for data
protection.

## 2. What we process, why, and on what basis

| What we do | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Employment and HR | To manage your employment: contract, manager, role, development, absence, rehabilitation, work environment, and the end of your employment | The employment contract; our legal duties under labour, work environment and social insurance law. Health data (sick leave, rehabilitation) only as employment and social insurance law requires |
| Payroll, expenses and benefits | To pay your salary and expenses, report tax and social fees, and run pension and insurance | The employment contract; our legal duties under tax and accounting law |
| Time reporting and staffing | To record your time, plan assignments and invoice customers | The employment contract; our customer contracts; accounting law; our legitimate interest in planning |
| IT accounts and equipment | To give you accounts, licences, access and equipment, and close them when you leave | The employment contract; our legitimate interest in running IT |
| Security logging | To protect our systems and investigate incidents: we log sign-ins and certain activity in Microsoft 365, Entra and Azure | Our legitimate interest in information security. Logs are not used to follow your work performance |
| Competence, CVs and certifications | To keep your CV and skills up to date, offer you for assignments, and record training and certifications | The employment contract; our legitimate interest in offering our consultants |
| Employee surveys | To understand engagement and wellbeing and support your development plan | Our legitimate interest in developing the workplace [to confirm: whether answers are anonymous] |
| Communication and collaboration | Mail, chat, meetings and files for your daily work | The employment contract; our legitimate interest in running the business |

## 3. What data

Your name, personal identity number, contact details and next of kin; employment terms, salary, bank account, tax and
benefits; unit, manager and job; absence and, where needed, sick leave and rehabilitation; time entries and
assignments; CV, skills, certifications and training; accounts, group memberships, equipment and sign-in logs; survey
answers; and what you write and store in our systems.

Most of it comes from you. Some comes from your manager, from our systems (logs, time reports), from Försäkringskassan
or from customers (for example feedback on an assignment).

## 4. Who gets your data

- **Inside Swedwise:** HR, your manager, finance and IT — only what each needs.
- **Authorities:** Skatteverket and Försäkringskassan, as the law requires.
- **Pension, insurance and occupational health providers** and our bank [to confirm: which].
- **Customers and partners:** your CV and relevant certifications when we offer you for an assignment.
- **Our suppliers (processors)**, who handle data for us under our instructions: Microsoft (Microsoft 365, Entra, Azure),
  Fortnox (payroll and accounting), Nexta (Nexta Hub), TimeLog (time, until we move to Nexta Hub), Winningtemp
  (surveys), LastPass (passwords), Slack (chat) and others listed in our records of processing.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some suppliers are US companies (for example Microsoft, Slack and LastPass). Where data may leave the EU/EEA, we rely
on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses [to confirm: per supplier]. You can ask us
for details.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Accounting and payroll records: **7 years** after the end of the calendar year, as the Swedish Accounting Act
  (Bokföringslagen) requires.
- Your employee file: during your employment and **2 years** after it ends [to confirm].
- Your Swedwise account is blocked on your last day and deleted **90 days** later.
- Your mailbox is converted to a shared mailbox for your manager when you leave, so that business mail is not lost
  [to confirm: how long it is kept]. Please do not keep private mail in your work mailbox.
- Security logs: about **12 months** [to confirm].
- Other data: as long as needed for its purpose, as set out in our records of processing.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have data erased when we no longer need it or have no right to keep it;
- have processing restricted while a question is examined;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- get data you gave us in a machine-readable format (data portability), where it applies.

Write to **privacy@swedwise.se**. We answer within one month. It is free.

## 8. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, tell us first at privacy@swedwise.se. You also have the right to complain to
the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**, www.imy.se.

## 9. Changes

We update this notice when our processing changes and tell you when we do.
