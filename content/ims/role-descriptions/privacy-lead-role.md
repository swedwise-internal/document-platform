# Role: Privacy Lead (Dataskyddsansvarig)

**Document ID**: SW-IMS-ROLE-006-v1.1

## Role Summary

The Privacy Lead is accountable for Swedwise AB's compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and
Swedish data protection law, as controller (employees, contacts, candidates) and as processor (customers' data in
consulting assignments and services). The Privacy Lead owns the data protection policy, the records of processing,
data protection impact assessments, data subject requests and the decision to notify a personal data breach.

## The Privacy Lead is not a Data Protection Officer

The Privacy Lead is **not** a Data Protection Officer (DPO) under GDPR Articles 37–39, and is not called one in any
document, notice or contract. A designated DPO, even a voluntary one, must be independent and may not hold a position
that decides the purposes and means of processing; a member of management cannot be one.

- **Assessment against Article 37:** Swedwise is not a public authority, and its core activities are neither
  large-scale regular and systematic monitoring of individuals nor large-scale processing of special categories of
  data. Swedwise is therefore not required to designate a DPO (decided by the owner 2026-10-29: «Vi måste inte ha ombud» (we do not have to have a DPO)). The assessment is recorded in the
  compliance obligation register (SW-IMS-PRO-013) and re-assessed yearly and whenever a new service or customer
  assignment changes the processing.
- **If the assessment changes:** Swedwise designates an external DPO, who reports to the CEO and is published as the
  contact point. The Privacy Lead remains the internal owner of data protection.

## Segregation from the CISO

The Privacy Lead and the CISO are **never held by the same person**. The owner, 2026-10-29: «Ciso och DPO får aldrig
vara samma» (the CISO and the DPO must never be the same person). The CISO (held by the COO, proposed) protects the
information; the Privacy Lead decides whether the processing of personal data is lawful and answers for it. A breach
is assessed by both, and the Privacy Lead decides on notification.

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Policy and accountability

- Own the Data Protection Policy (SW-ISMS-POL-004) and keep it current
- Keep the records of processing activities (ROPA) for Swedwise as controller and as processor
- Make sure every processing activity has a lawful basis and a retention period
- Report data protection status to the management review

### 2. Agreements

- Make sure data processing agreements exist with every processor Swedwise uses (with the CFO for ordinary suppliers
  and the CCO for partners and vendors)
- Review the data processing terms in customer contracts where Swedwise is processor (with the CCO and the CSO)

### 3. Impact assessments and privacy by design

- Decide when a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is needed, and approve it
- Make sure new services, systems and customer assignments consider data protection from the start

### 4. Data subjects and the supervisory authority

- Own the handling of data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, objection, portability) within one month
- Be the contact point for Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) and for data subjects
- Contact address: dataskydd@swedwise.se (proposed shared mailbox; members: CEO and CFO)

### 5. Personal data breaches

- Assess every reported personal data breach together with the CISO
- Decide whether to notify IMY (within 72 hours) and the data subjects, and make the notification
- Inform affected customers without undue delay where Swedwise is processor

### 6. Awareness

- Own the content of the GDPR and data protection training (SW-IMS-TRN-004)
- Answer employees' data protection questions

## Authority

The Privacy Lead has authority to:

- Stop or pause a processing activity that lacks a lawful basis or a required DPIA
- Require a data processing agreement before a processor is used
- Request any information needed about processing activities from all units
- Decide on breach notifications to IMY and data subjects

| **CFO** (stand-in) | Data processing agreements with ordinary suppliers; payroll and finance data; stand-in during absence | Quarterly and during absence |

| **CSO** | Customers' personal data in consulting assignments | As needed |
| **Risk Manager** | Privacy risks in the risk register | Quarterly |
| **IMY** | Breach notifications, inquiries, prior consultation | As needed |

## Performance Indicators

| KPI | Target | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|
| Data subject requests answered within one month | 100% | Request log |
| Breach notifications to IMY within 72 hours, when required | 100% | Breach register |
| High-risk processing with a DPIA | 100% | DPIA register |
| Processing activities in the ROPA | 100% | ROPA review |
| Processors with a valid data processing agreement | 100% | Supplier and partner registers |

## Delegation and Backup

The **CFO** is the Privacy Lead's stand-in (owner 2026-10-29) and takes over during absence, including the decision on
breach notifications within the 72 hours. The stand-in is never the CISO.
