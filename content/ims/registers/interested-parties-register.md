## 1. Purpose

This register records the parties that can affect, be affected by, or perceive themselves to be affected by Swedwise AB's decisions and activities, their needs and expectations, which of those Swedwise has decided to treat as compliance obligations, how each is monitored, and which job owns the relationship. It supports the context of the organization (ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO/IEC 27001, clause 4.2) as set out in SW-IMS-MAN-004 section 3 and is maintained under SW-IMS-PRO-011.

## 2. How to Read the Register

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **ID** | Stable register id (IP-nn) |
| **Party** | The interested party or group |
| **Needs and expectations** | What the party needs or expects from Swedwise that is relevant to the IMS |
| **Compliance obligation** | **Legal** (law or regulation), **Contract** (agreed in a contract or programme), **Adopted** (Swedwise has chosen to comply), **No** (an expectation we take into account but are not bound by) |
| **IMS** | QMS, EMS, ISMS or All |
| **Power / Interest** | Power-interest prioritization (SW-IMS-PRO-011): High / Medium / Low |
| **Monitoring** | How Swedwise follows the party's needs and whether they are met |
| **Owner (job)** | The job that owns the relationship and the entry |

Compliance obligations marked Legal, Contract or Adopted are carried into the compliance obligations list kept under SW-IMS-PRO-013 and into the integrated risk register (SW-IMS-REG-001).

## 5. Review and Update

This register is reviewed:
- **Annually** in the comprehensive context analysis (Q4), SW-IMS-PRO-011
- **Quarterly** in management review (changes in parties or their requirements)
- **When triggered**: new customer segment or major contract, new partner programme, new office, new law or regulation, change in ownership, new AI use, change in scope

Changes are recorded in the document control table below. Obligations that change are passed to SW-IMS-PRO-013 and risks to SW-IMS-REG-001.
