Superseded by SW-IMS-PRO-005 — proposal 2026-10-29, for the owner's check.

## 1. Why this procedure is superseded

SW-IMS-PRO-005 Nonconformity and Corrective Action Procedure and this procedure described the same process in
two ways: SW-IMS-PRO-005 as eight steps (identify, record, contain, analyse, plan, implement, verify, close),
this one as the 8D method (D0–D8), with different response times, different form numbers and a different
register. Two procedures for one process cannot both be followed.

## 2. Where the content went

| What this procedure held | Where it is now |
|--------------------------|-----------------|
| The corrective action process | SW-IMS-PRO-005 section 6, Steps 1–8 |
| The 8D method (team, 5W2H problem description, root-cause methods, prevent recurrence) | SW-IMS-PRO-005 Step 4, as the method for **major and critical** nonconformities |
| Root-cause methods: 5 Whys, Fishbone (6M), Barrier Analysis | SW-IMS-PRO-005 Step 4 and Appendices A–B |
| Classification and response times | SW-IMS-PRO-005 section 5 (its times apply) |
| CAR form | SW-IMS-FRM-003 Corrective Action Request (CAR) Form, referenced from SW-IMS-PRO-005 |
| Metrics | SW-IMS-PRO-005 section 9 |

Use SW-IMS-PRO-005. Documents that still reference SW-IMS-PRO-009 should be changed to reference
SW-IMS-PRO-005 when they are next revised.
