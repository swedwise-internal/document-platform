**Document ID**: SW-IMS-GAP-ALL-v1.0

## Executive Summary

This consolidated gap analysis assesses Swedwise AB's readiness for integrated certification against all three management system standards. The analysis reveals that while a foundational IMS framework has been established, significant work remains before certification readiness.

### Overall Conformance Assessment

| Standard | Current Conformance | Gap | Estimated Time to Readiness |
|----------|-------------------|-----|---------------------------|
| ISO 9001:2015 (Quality) | ~25% | 75% | 4-6 months |
| ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental) | ~25% | 75% | 10-12 months |
| ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security) | ~15% | 85% | 9-12 months |
| **Integrated IMS** | **~20%** | **80%** | **10-12 months** |

### Key Strengths

1. **Integrated Management System framework** established - efficient approach
2. **IMS Policy** documented with commitments across all three standards
3. **Document Control Procedure** in place and well-structured
4. **Context of Organization** comprehensively documented
5. **IMS Awareness Training** module created (needs delivery)
6. **Project Plan** with clear phases and deliverables identified
7. **Learning organization culture** ("The Machine") supports adoption

## Critical Success Factors

1. **Executive Commitment**: CEO and management team must actively support and participate
2. **Role Assignment**: Fill CISO and IMS Lead roles immediately
3. **Resource Allocation**: Dedicate time and budget - this is not a part-time activity
4. **Phased Approach**: Don't try to do everything at once
5. **Integration Focus**: Leverage common requirements to reduce duplication
6. **Practical Controls**: Design processes that fit a 35-person company
7. **Staff Engagement**: Communicate the "why" and involve staff
8. **Evidence Collection**: Start collecting evidence early
9. **External Support**: Consider consultants for specialized areas (especially 27001 risk assessment)
10. **SaaS Priority**: Given business direction, prioritize security controls for SaaS

## Conclusion

Swedwise AB has established a solid foundation for an Integrated Management System but requires substantial work to achieve certification readiness across all three standards.

**Key Message**: The integrated approach is efficient and appropriate for Swedwise's size. However, ISO 27001 represents the largest effort due to the 93 Annex A controls and technical security requirements. Given Swedwise's SaaS ambitions and customer requirements, ISO 27001 should be prioritized alongside the other standards.

**Timeline**: 10-12 months to integrated certification is achievable with dedicated resources and management commitment.

**Critical Path**: CISO appointment → Risk assessment → Statement of Applicability → Security control implementation → Internal audit → Certification

The investment in IMS certification will enable Swedwise to:
- Meet public procurement requirements
- Demonstrate commitment to quality, environment, and security
- Compete effectively for larger contracts
- Reduce operational risks
- Drive continual improvement
