# IMS Implementation Status Report

**Report Date**: 2025-12-15
**Project Phase**: Phase 2 - Design (Policies and Procedures)
**Overall Status**: **At Risk**
**Document ID**: SW-IMS-STATUS-2025-12-15

---

## Executive Summary

Swedwise AB has made meaningful progress establishing the foundation for an Integrated Management System (IMS). Core framework documents are in place, including the IMS Policy, Document Control Procedure, Context of Organization, and Risk Assessment Procedure. Several key deliverables for ISO 27001 and ISO 14001 have been drafted.

However, the project remains **at risk** due to:
1. **No IMS roles assigned** to individuals (CISO, IMS Owner, etc.)
2. **No risk assessments conducted** - templates exist but no workshops held
3. **Training not delivered** - awareness module created but staff not trained
4. **Statement of Applicability (SoA)** not created for ISO 27001
5. **Many critical procedures missing** (Internal Audit, Management Review, Incident Management, etc.)

**Current Conformance**: ~20% overall (per gap analysis dated 2025-12-15)

**Estimated Time to Certification**: 10-12 months with dedicated resources

## Gap Analysis Status

A consolidated gap analysis was completed on 2025-12-15, documenting:

| Standard | Current Conformance | Gap | Estimated Time |
|----------|-------------------|-----|----------------|
| ISO 9001:2015 | ~25% | 75% | 4-6 months |
| ISO 14001:2015 | ~25% | 75% | 10-12 months |
| ISO 27001:2022 | ~15% | 85% | 9-12 months |
| **Integrated IMS** | **~20%** | **80%** | **10-12 months** |

### Critical Gaps Identified

1. **CISO not appointed** - No one accountable for information security
2. **No risk assessment conducted** - Procedure exists but no workshop held
3. **Statement of Applicability missing** - Mandatory ISO 27001 requirement
4. **No internal audit capability** - Procedure, training, and schedule needed
5. **No management review conducted** - Required to demonstrate management commitment
6. **Training not delivered** - Material exists but staff not trained
