## Next Steps and Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (1-2 Weeks)

1. **Create IMS Manual** (CRITICAL)
   - Structure: 7 sections aligned with ISO HLS (clauses 4-10)
   - Content: Reference all existing policies and procedures
   - Include: Process interaction diagram, scope statement
   - Assign: IMS Owner
   - Effort: 2-3 days

2. **Populate [TBD] Placeholders**
   - Organize workshops with department heads
   - Gather organizational data: names, dates, metrics, objectives
   - Update all 106 documents systematically
   - Assign: IMS Owner + Management Team
   - Effort: 3-5 days

3. **Conduct Management Review**
   - Present IMS documentation to management
   - Obtain approval for core policies
   - Establish IMS objectives and targets
   - Assign roles (IMS Owner, CISO, Quality Lead, Environmental Lead)
   - Assign: CEO + Management Team
   - Effort: 1 day meeting + preparation

4. **Establish Document Approval Workflow**
   - Define approval authority matrix
   - Set up approval tracking (web platform or manual)
   - Approve and publish core policies
   - Set effective dates
   - Assign: IMS Owner
   - Effort: 2 days

### Short-Term Actions (3-6 Weeks)

5. **Populate Risk Register**
   - Conduct risk assessment workshop (Section 6 of Risk Assessment Procedure)
   - Identify top 20-30 risks across quality, environmental, security
   - Assign risk owners and treatment plans
   - Assign: IMS Owner, CISO, Quality Lead, Environmental Lead
   - Effort: 2 days workshop + follow-up

6. **Populate Asset Register**
   - Inventory critical information assets
   - Assign asset owners and classifications
   - Document existing controls
   - Assign: CISO + IT Manager
   - Effort: 2 days

7. **Deliver Core Training**
   - IMS Awareness Training (all staff)
   - Information Security Awareness (all staff)
   - Role-specific training (managers, internal auditors)
   - Assign: IMS Owner, CISO
   - Effort: 3-5 days preparation + delivery

8. **Conduct Internal Audit (Cycle 1)**
   - Plan audit scope and schedule
   - Train internal auditors
   - Execute audit of 3-5 key processes
   - Document findings and corrective actions
   - Assign: Internal Auditors
   - Effort: 5 days

### Medium-Term Actions (2-3 Months)

9. **Implement LMS Integration**
   - Build training course structure in web platform
   - Implement assessment and tracking
   - Migrate training content
   - Assign: Web Platform Team
   - Effort: 5-10 days

10. **Execute Evidence Collection**
    - Implement records management system
    - Collect evidence for ISO requirements
    - Organize for external audit
    - Assign: IMS Owner
    - Effort: Ongoing, 3 days initial setup

11. **Conduct Pre-Certification Readiness Assessment**
    - Internal gap analysis against ISO standards
    - Mock audit by external consultant (optional)
    - Address final gaps
    - Assign: IMS Owner + External Consultant
    - Effort: 3-5 days

12. **Schedule Certification Audit**
    - Select certification body
    - Schedule Stage 1 (documentation review) and Stage 2 (on-site audit)
    - Prepare audit evidence package
    - Assign: IMS Owner + Management
    - Effort: 2 days preparation
