## 1. Introduction

This document defines the context of Swedwise AB as required by ISO 9001 (Clause 4), ISO 14001 (Clause 4), and ISO 27001 (Clause 4). Understanding our context ensures the IMS is appropriate to our purpose, strategic direction, and operating environment.
