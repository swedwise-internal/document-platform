# IMS Awareness Training

**Module ID**: SW-TRN-001
**Duration**: 30 minutes
**Target Audience**: All Swedwise employees
**Prerequisites**: None

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this training, you will be able to:
1. Explain what an Integrated Management System (IMS) is and why Swedwise has one
2. Identify your role and responsibilities within the IMS
3. Recognize situations where IMS policies and procedures apply
4. Know where to find IMS documentation and who to contact for help

## Introduction: Why Does This Matter?

### The Short Version

Swedwise is pursuing ISO certification in three areas:
- **Quality** (ISO 9001) - Delivering excellent service consistently
- **Environment** (ISO 14001) - Being responsible about our environmental impact
- **Information Security** (ISO 27001) - Protecting data and systems

**Why?** Because our customers - especially public sector organizations - increasingly require these certifications. It's also simply good business practice.

### Make Time For The Good

Our brand promise applies here too. A well-run IMS helps us:
- Spend less time fixing problems
- Have clear processes instead of reinventing the wheel
- Build customer trust
- Create a better working environment

## What Is an IMS?

An Integrated Management System is simply a structured way of:
- Documenting how we work
- Managing risks
- Measuring performance
- Continuously improving

Think of it as the "operating manual" for Swedwise.

### The PDCA Cycle

Everything in the IMS follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle:

```
    ┌─────────┐
    │  PLAN   │ ─── Set objectives, plan how to achieve them
    └────┬────┘
         ▼
    ┌─────────┐
    │   DO    │ ─── Implement the plan
    └────┬────┘
         ▼
    ┌─────────┐
    │  CHECK  │ ─── Monitor and measure results
    └────┬────┘
         ▼
    ┌─────────┐
    │   ACT   │ ─── Take action to improve
    └────┬────┘
         │
         └──────▶ Back to PLAN (continuous loop)
```

## The Three Pillars

### Quality (ISO 9001)

**Focus**: Customer satisfaction and service delivery

**Your role**:
- Understand customer requirements
- Follow documented processes
- Report issues and suggestions for improvement
- Participate in customer feedback

### Environment (ISO 14001)

**Focus**: Minimizing our environmental impact

**Your role**:
- Be mindful of energy use (turn off lights, equipment)
- Minimize travel where virtual meetings work
- Proper waste disposal and recycling
- Consider environmental factors in procurement

### Information Security (ISO 27001)

**Focus**: Protecting information and systems

**Your role**:
- Protect passwords and access credentials
- Handle customer data appropriately
- Report security incidents
- Follow security policies when working remotely

## Your Responsibilities

Everyone at Swedwise is responsible for:

| Area | What This Means |
|------|-----------------|
| **Following Policies** | Read and comply with IMS policies relevant to your role |
| **Using Procedures** | Follow documented procedures for key activities |
| **Reporting Issues** | Report non-conformities, incidents, and near-misses |
| **Suggesting Improvements** | Share ideas for making things work better |
| **Maintaining Records** | Complete required documentation accurately |
| **Staying Competent** | Complete required training, keep skills current |

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Working at a Client Site

You're consulting at a customer location. What IMS aspects apply?

- **Security**: Follow Swedwise security policies AND customer security rules
- **Quality**: Deliver work according to agreed specifications
- **Environment**: Be mindful of travel and resource use

**Key action**: If there's a conflict between our policies and the customer's, escalate to your manager.

### Scenario 2: You Notice Something Wrong

You discover that a process isn't being followed, or you see a potential security issue.

**Key action**: Report it! Use the incident reporting process. Early reporting prevents small issues becoming big problems.

### Scenario 3: A Customer Asks About Our Certifications

A customer asks if Swedwise is ISO certified.

**Key action**: Direct them to our IMS Owner or use the official statement. Don't make claims about certification status unless you're certain.

### Scenario 4: You're Not Sure What to Do

You encounter a situation not covered by any procedure you know.

**Key action**: Ask! Check with your manager or the IMS Owner. It's better to ask than to guess.

## Quick Reference: The Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Follow documented procedures
- Report incidents and near-misses promptly
- Keep your training up to date
- Ask questions when unsure
- Suggest improvements
- Protect passwords and sensitive information

### Don't:
- Ignore security policies "just this once"
- Assume someone else will report an issue
- Create workarounds without proper approval
- Share confidential information inappropriately
- Disable security controls for convenience
