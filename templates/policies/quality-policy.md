## 1. Purpose

This Quality Policy establishes Swedwise AB's commitment to delivering high-quality services that meet or exceed customer expectations. It provides the framework for setting quality objectives and demonstrates our dedication to continual improvement as an integral part of our business operations.

This policy supports our ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System and directly embodies our brand promise: **"Make Time For The Good"** — by delivering quality services efficiently, we create time for what truly matters to our customers.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **All Swedwise employees**, contractors, and temporary staff
- **All organizational locations**: Karlstad (HQ), Stockholm, Uddevalla, and remote workers
- **All service delivery activities**, including:
  - Software licensing and sales
  - Implementation and consulting services
  - System integration and development
  - Managed services and support
  - SaaS platform delivery
- **All customer interactions** from initial contact through ongoing support

---

## 3. Quality Commitment Statement

Swedwise AB is committed to providing high-quality services that consistently meet customer requirements and exceed expectations. We believe that quality is the foundation of our business and essential to our success.

**Our quality commitment means:**

- Understanding what our customers truly need
- Delivering what we promise, when we promise it
- Getting it right the first time
- Continuously learning and improving
- Taking ownership and accountability for outcomes

---

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Customer Focus

Swedwise commits to understanding and meeting customer needs:

- **Listen and understand**: We actively engage with customers to understand their requirements, challenges, and expectations before, during, and after service delivery
- **Clear communication**: We communicate openly and honestly about what we can deliver, timelines, and any issues that arise
- **Deliver value**: We focus on outcomes that create real value for our customers, not just completing tasks
- **Measure satisfaction**: We systematically collect and act on customer feedback to drive improvement

### 4.2 Service Excellence

We are committed to delivering services that meet the highest professional standards:

- **Competent consultants**: Our staff have the knowledge, skills, and experience to deliver excellent results
- **Proven methodologies**: We use structured approaches to project delivery, adapted to customer context
- **Quality deliverables**: All work products meet defined quality standards before delivery
- **Reliable support**: We provide responsive and effective support when customers need help

### 4.3 Process-Based Approach

Swedwise manages quality through well-defined processes:

- **Documented processes**: Key activities are documented to ensure consistency and enable improvement
- **Clear accountability**: Process owners are responsible for process effectiveness
- **Measured performance**: We track key indicators to understand how well our processes work
- **Risk-based thinking**: We identify and address risks that could affect service quality

### 4.4 Continuous Improvement

We are committed to getting better every day:

- **Learn from experience**: We review project outcomes and capture lessons learned
- **Act on feedback**: Customer feedback and complaints drive process improvements
- **Invest in people**: We develop our staff's competencies through training and knowledge sharing
- **Innovate**: We explore new technologies and approaches to improve customer outcomes
- **PDCA cycle**: We apply Plan-Do-Check-Act thinking to all improvement activities

### 4.5 Partnership and Collaboration

Quality is achieved through effective partnerships:

- **Supplier quality**: We select and manage suppliers who share our quality commitment
- **Customer collaboration**: We work with customers as partners, not just service providers
- **Internal teamwork**: We collaborate across teams and offices to deliver the best outcomes
- **Knowledge sharing**: We share expertise through our discipline forums and learning practices

### 4.6 Compliance and Standards

We meet all applicable requirements:

- **Customer requirements**: We fulfill contractual commitments and customer specifications
- **Regulatory requirements**: We comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **Industry standards**: We follow recognized best practices in our areas of expertise
- **ISO 9001**: We maintain a QMS that conforms to ISO 9001:2015 requirements

## 5. Quality Objectives Framework

Swedwise establishes measurable quality objectives aligned with this policy. Our focus areas include:

### Key Quality Indicators

| Objective Area | Example Metrics |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Customer Satisfaction** | Customer satisfaction score, Net Promoter Score (NPS) |
| **On-Time Delivery** | Percentage of projects delivered on schedule |
| **Service Quality** | First-time-right rate, defect rates, rework |
| **Support Responsiveness** | Response times, resolution times, SLA compliance |
| **Competence** | Training completion, certification rates |
| **Improvement** | Number of improvements implemented, customer suggestions actioned |

### Objective Setting Criteria

Quality objectives shall be:
- **Consistent** with this Quality Policy
- **Measurable** with defined targets
- **Monitored** regularly (at least quarterly)
- **Communicated** to relevant staff
- **Updated** annually or when circumstances change

Specific objectives, targets, and action plans are documented in the **Objectives and Targets Register**.

- Escalating quality issues appropriately

- Delivering work that meets customer requirements
- Following documented processes and procedures
- Reporting quality issues and improvement opportunities

- Participating in training and development activities

- Taking responsibility for the quality of their work
- Reporting issues, concerns, and improvement ideas
- Participating in quality training and awareness activities
- Treating customer satisfaction as a personal priority

## 9. Review and Revision

### Review Frequency

This Quality Policy is reviewed:
- At least annually during Management Review
- When significant changes occur in:
  - Business operations or strategic direction
  - Customer requirements or expectations
  - Quality performance or issues
  - ISO 9001 requirements
- Following audit findings or major non-conformities

### Review Responsibility

- **Primary**: Quality Lead
- **Approval**: CEO / Top Management
- **Consultation**: Management Team

### Change Control

Changes to this policy:
- Are documented with version control
- Are approved by Top Management
- Are communicated to all affected parties
- Trigger review of related QMS documents

## 10. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Quality** | The degree to which a set of inherent characteristics fulfills requirements |
| **Customer** | Organization or person that receives a product or service |
| **Customer satisfaction** | Customer's perception of the degree to which their expectations have been fulfilled |
| **Nonconformity** | Non-fulfillment of a requirement |
| **Corrective action** | Action to eliminate the cause of a nonconformity and prevent recurrence |
| **Continual improvement** | Recurring activity to enhance performance |
| **Process** | Set of interrelated activities that transform inputs into outputs |
| **QMS** | Quality Management System - part of a management system with regard to quality |
