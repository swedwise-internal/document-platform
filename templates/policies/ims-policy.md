## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Swedwise's commitment to maintaining an Integrated Management System (IMS) that ensures excellence in quality, environmental responsibility, and information security. It provides the framework for setting and reviewing objectives across all three management system domains.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Swedwise employees, contractors, and temporary staff
- All locations (Karlstad, Stockholm, Uddevalla)
- All business activities including consulting, software licensing, and SaaS service delivery
- All information and assets owned or managed by Swedwise

## 3. Policy Statement

Swedwise is committed to:

### Quality (ISO 9001)
- Delivering services that consistently meet customer requirements and expectations
- Understanding and fulfilling customer needs throughout the service lifecycle
- Continuously improving our processes to enhance customer satisfaction
- Making time for what matters most to our customers through efficiency and automation

### Environmental Responsibility (ISO 14001)
- Operating in an environmentally responsible manner
- Preventing pollution and minimizing our environmental footprint
- Complying with applicable environmental legislation and regulations
- Promoting sustainable practices in our operations and supply chain

### Information Security (ISO 27001)
- Protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information
- Managing information security risks systematically
- Complying with legal, regulatory, and contractual security requirements
- Building and maintaining trust with our customers through robust security practices

### Integration and Continuous Improvement
- Operating an integrated management system that delivers efficiency and consistency
- Making risk-based decisions that consider quality, environmental, and security impacts
- Engaging all employees in continual improvement of our processes
- Setting measurable objectives and monitoring performance

## 4. Commitments

Swedwise Management Team commits to:

1. **Leadership**: Providing visible leadership and support for the IMS
2. **Resources**: Ensuring adequate resources for IMS implementation and maintenance
3. **Competence**: Ensuring all personnel are competent for their IMS responsibilities
4. **Communication**: Communicating the importance of the IMS to all stakeholders
5. **Review**: Regularly reviewing IMS performance and driving improvement
6. **Compliance**: Meeting all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
7. **Risk Management**: Maintaining a risk-based approach to all management system activities

## 5. Objectives

Specific, measurable objectives will be established annually for:
- Customer satisfaction and service quality
- Environmental performance and sustainability
- Information security and data protection
- Process efficiency and improvement

These objectives will be documented in the Objectives and Targets Register and reviewed quarterly.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| CEO | Overall accountability for the IMS |
| IMS Owner | Day-to-day management and coordination of the IMS |
| CISO | Leadership of information security management |

| Environmental Lead | Coordination of environmental management activities |

| All Employees | Following policies and procedures, reporting issues |
